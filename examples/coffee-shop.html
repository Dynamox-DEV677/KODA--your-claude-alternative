Our Craft

Three pillars.
One obsession.

We don't believe in shortcuts. Every cup starts with a decision made weeks before the bean reaches our roaster.

🌱

Single-Origin Beans

We source directly from smallholder farms in Araku Valley, Coorg, and Chikmagalur. Each lot is traceable to the estate — you'll know exactly where your coffee slept last night.

🔥

Small-Batch Roasting

Every Tuesday, we roast 12 kg batches on a refurbished Probat drum. We profile each bean individually — light for floral Ethiopians, medium for chocolaty South Indians.

🫗

Pour-Over Bar

No espresso machines. No automation. Every cup is hand-poured to order — V60, Kalita, or Aeropress. We weigh, bloom, and pour. It takes four minutes. It's worth it.

Our Story

Two friends,
one roaster,
a city that needed better coffee.

Arun and Meera met at a cupping lab in Bengaluru in 2019. He was a chemical engineer who'd spent two years building a fluid-bed roaster in his garage. She was a barista who'd left a corporate job to chase the perfect pour-over.

They spent the next three years travelling to coffee estates across South India — sleeping on plantation floors, cupping hundreds of lots, learning why the same bean tastes different when grown 200 metres higher.

"We wanted a place where the coffee was the point. Not the Wi-Fi. Not the ambience. Just the cup."

Ember & Oak opened in December 2024 in a quiet lane off TTK Road, Chennai. No signboard out front — just the smell of freshly roasted beans drifting onto the street. The roaster sits in the window. The pour-over bar is the centrepiece. There are exactly twelve seats.
